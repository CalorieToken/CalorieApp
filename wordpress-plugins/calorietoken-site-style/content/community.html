No active voting

This hub introduces our community participation plans and preserves earlier ideas. There is currently no active vote, proposal submission or voting function.

Any future programme will need clear participation rules, a defined decision process and its own technical, privacy and legal review. No launch date or reward is promised.

A place for future participation

We want to make the project's direction easier to understand and, when the foundations are ready, give the community a clear way to contribute. The current priority is the CalorieApp food experience and preparation for showcases.
