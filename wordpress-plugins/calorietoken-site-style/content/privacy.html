Website, CalorieApp, CalDB and CalEco features: September 2026

Language and the help guide

The website can remember your selected display language on your device and share that preference with the embedded CalorieApp. This preference is separate from signing in. The Calorie Help guide matches questions against bundled project information in your browser; it does not create a support ticket or email, or send your question to an external AI service. Avoid entering personal, medical, recovery-phrase or other wallet-secret information in it.

Accounts, profiles and wallets

A CalorieApp account is separate from an XRPL wallet. An account can be designated Consumer, Business or Both. Connecting a wallet is optional and is only needed for wallet-specific features. Profile and business-directory functions can be expanded in stages; information intended for a future searchable public profile, menu or business listing must be treated as public only when the user explicitly chooses to publish it. Private account records are not made public merely because an account also has a wallet.

Food sources, CalDB and contributions

CalorieApp can combine attributed food and source information from multiple datasets. Each external dataset remains subject to its own source, licence, coverage and accuracy limitations. Food-source records remain separate from a user's private food history.

Where CalDB or CalEco public interfaces are provided, they are intended only for food, source, provenance or contribution data that is explicitly designated as public. Private food logs, receipts, purchase history, inventory, sales, expenses and wallet-linked private records are not exposed through a public API simply because the wider ecosystem can combine shared data. A purchase or payment record is never automatically treated as proof that the food was eaten.

POS, payment and Testnet pilots

POS, inventory, sales/expense tracking and payment-request functions are voluntary staged pilots. Practice data, Testnet activity and any later live mode must remain distinguishable. XRPL transactions can have public ledger transaction hashes. Cash, card or external fiat-provider payments do not receive a fabricated XRPL hash. External payment providers process the information required by their own service under their own notices and terms.

The optional Testnet guide uses test-only XRP Ledger tools. Testnet is separate from Mainnet, can reset and has no real monetary value. Never use a real wallet recovery phrase or send real funds to a test address. Creating a Testnet account does not automatically create a CalorieApp session or move an existing private food history.

Account controls and retention

Where available, an authenticated private export downloads the user's own application records to their device; downloading does not itself delete the server copy or transmit the export to another service. Erasure, import and migration functions remain separately controlled. Erasing CalorieApp records cannot erase public ledger records or data independently retained by WordPress, a wallet provider, payment provider or food-data source where that party has its own lawful basis or retention duty.

The planned inactive-account policy is 24 months of inactivity with a 30-day advance warning. Any automatic warning or enforcement must be enabled and verified before it is relied upon. The engineering target for encrypted-backup retention after erasure is at most 30 days, subject to provider and restore verification. Ask through the privacy contact above about an applicable request or retention requirement.

External media, markets and your choices

X, YouTube, XPMarket, Xaman and AllChainBridge/SWFT are separate external services. An external link does not load the destination until you open it; an allowed embed can transmit technical data to that provider. Cookie choices continue to apply where consent is required. Public information can be browsed without creating an account; saving private logs or using account-backed features requires the information necessary to provide those functions.
