Current integrations and community information

The CAL & Crypto website page contains three separate sections: CAL/XRP routes to external services, a buying and selling guide, and the AllChainBridge/SWFT interface. The current release does not operate its own order-execution engine. The user reviews any external transaction in their own wallet. Xaman signing alone does not establish an exemption from financial-services rules. Provider availability, asset support and eligibility depend on the provider and jurisdiction.

CAL is temporarily unavailable in the displayed SWFT integration. Other supported assets or routes to XRP may be available; check the actual interface, network, recipient, fees, limits and provider terms before proceeding. CalorieApp food search and logging do not require buying CAL.

The optional Testnet guide uses test-only tools. Test assets have no real monetary value and networks can reset. A test account is not a Mainnet account, an investment, a reward entitlement or a promise of participation in a future pilot. Creating one does not bypass CalorieApp authentication or grant access to another account's records.

The Community Voting Hub is an informational page. No vote, proposal submission or community voting function is active. Historical reward illustrations describe an earlier concept, not an approved pool, allocated reward, claimable entitlement or present commitment. Any future programme needs its own published rules and review.

The Calorie Help guide supplies bounded project information and links. It does not provide personalised legal, investment or medical advice, operate customer support automatically or make commitments for the operator. Documentation and provider information can change; consult the dated source appropriate to your question.

Open Food Facts and the dated USDA FoodData Central catalogue and reference selection have separate source and licensing notices. Their inclusion does not guarantee complete nutrition data or accuracy for a particular product or person. USDA records identify their collection and edition; the historical SR Legacy collection is not a statement of current product availability. Existing third-party licences and mandatory consumer rights remain in effect.

Similar-food suggestions are ideas for comparing records based on names, not medical or personalised dietary advice or a guarantee of suitability. Check ingredients, allergens, raw or cooked preparation, units and reference quantity against the actual food. Gram calculations concern the edible amount in the selected record. Missing values are not zero, and no Nutri-Score is invented for USDA records. You decide which food and portion to confirm in your diary.

Free app phase and pilot preparation

Available CalorieApp functions are currently free of subscription and CalorieApp transaction charges. External network or provider fees may still apply. There is no automatic paid subscription, hidden trial end date or fee accumulated for later collection. Any future optional premium service or platform fee will require a clear new offer and explicit acceptance, with applicable price, tax, cancellation and refund information. Your existing free use is not consent to future charges.

A voluntary merchant and consumer pilot is being prepared but is not currently available. Planned inventory, purchase expenses, real-token payments and a separate practice environment must not be treated as live services. Both parties would need to choose participation; any diary entry would require a separate portion confirmation. Buying food does not mean that it has been eaten. No token purchase is required for food search or existing diary functions.

Recipe nutrition is an estimate from stated ingredients and quantities. The 640 named recipe choices consist of base recipes and measured variants; generated food illustrations are examples, not exact product or recipe photographs. Food comparisons and recorded labels are not medical advice or independent guarantees of health, safety, environmental impact or animal welfare. Check the actual ingredients, allergens, source and serving basis. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.
