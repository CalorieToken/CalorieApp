Current integrations, pilots and community information

The CAL & Crypto page provides guidance and links to external XRPL or cross-network services. The website does not operate its own order-execution engine. Users review any external transaction or signing request themselves. Provider availability, asset support, identity checks, limits, fees and eligibility depend on the provider and jurisdiction. CalorieApp food search and personal food logging do not require buying CAL or connecting a wallet.

A CalorieApp account is separate from an XRPL wallet and may be designated Consumer, Business or Both. A wallet is optional except for wallet-specific functions. Searchable merchant profiles, menus, inventory, offers, opening hours, dine-in, takeaway, delivery and farmer-to-consumer discovery are staged product directions and must not be treated as an available commercial service until the relevant feature is actually released.

POS, inventory, sales/expense tracking and payment-request functions are voluntary pilots. Practice, Testnet and any later live payment mode must be clearly distinguishable. Test assets have no real monetary value. A payment or purchase does not automatically create a food-consumption log. XRPL transaction hashes refer to validated ledger transactions; cash, card and external fiat-provider transactions must not be represented as XRPL transactions when they are not.

Where CalDB or CalEco public interfaces are provided, they are limited to data explicitly designated for public food, source, provenance or contribution use. Private food logs, receipts, inventory, sales, expenses and wallet-linked private records remain private account data unless the user takes a separate, explicit action to publish permitted information. Availability of shared data does not guarantee completeness, provenance, accuracy or suitability for a particular decision.

Nutrition values, comparisons, alternatives and recipe suggestions are informational tools. They are not medical diagnosis, treatment or personalised diet prescriptions. Users with medical, allergy or other high-stakes dietary requirements should verify relevant source information and seek an appropriately qualified professional where needed.

The Community Voting Hub is informational unless an active voting function is separately announced. Historical reward illustrations are not an approved pool, allocated reward, claimable entitlement or present commitment. Any future reward, fee, premium, marketplace or community programme requires its own published rules and applicable review.

The Calorie Help guide supplies bounded project information and links. It does not provide personalised legal, investment or medical advice, operate customer support automatically or make commitments for the operator. Documentation and provider information can change; consult the dated source appropriate to the question.

External food, price, recipe, knowledge and payment datasets or services retain their own licences, terms and limitations. Their inclusion does not guarantee complete or error-free data. Existing third-party licences and mandatory consumer rights remain in effect.
