Current integrations and community information

The CAL & Crypto website page contains three separate sections: CAL/XRP routes to external services, a buying and selling guide, and the AllChainBridge/SWFT interface. The current release does not operate its own order-execution engine. The user reviews any external transaction in their own wallet. Xaman signing alone does not establish an exemption from financial-services rules. Provider availability, asset support and eligibility depend on the provider and jurisdiction.

CAL is temporarily unavailable in the displayed SWFT integration. Other supported assets or routes to XRP may be available; check the actual interface, network, recipient, fees, limits and provider terms before proceeding. CalorieApp food search and logging do not require buying CAL.

The optional Testnet guide uses test-only tools. Test assets have no real monetary value and networks can reset. A test account is not a Mainnet account, an investment, a reward entitlement or a promise of participation in a future pilot. Creating one does not bypass CalorieApp authentication or grant access to another account's records.

The Community Voting Hub is an informational page. No vote, proposal submission or community voting function is active. Historical reward illustrations describe an earlier concept, not an approved pool, allocated reward, claimable entitlement or present commitment. Any future programme needs its own published rules and review.

The Calorie Help guide supplies bounded project information and links. It does not provide personalised legal, investment or medical advice, operate customer support automatically or make commitments for the operator. Documentation and provider information can change; consult the dated source appropriate to your question.

Open Food Facts and the small USDA FoodData Central reference selection have separate source and licensing notices. Their inclusion does not guarantee complete nutrition data or accuracy for a particular product or person. Existing third-party licences and mandatory consumer rights remain in effect.
