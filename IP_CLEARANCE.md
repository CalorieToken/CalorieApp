# Intellectual-property boundary

This repository publishes reviewed application expression; it does not claim ownership of general ideas such as calorie tracking, food search, external identity or distributed-data concepts.

Contributions require documented provenance and compatible rights. Dependencies, datasets, images, names and marks retain their own licences and restrictions. Open Food Facts reuse must preserve the attribution and database-licence requirements described in `DATA_LICENSING.md`.

A fresh legal and technical review is required before adding financial execution, wallet custody, regulated claims, bulk datasets, biometric or health profiling, new branding, restrictive SDKs or copied third-party material. This document is an engineering boundary, not legal advice or a freedom-to-operate opinion.
