# Asset provenance

Visual assets distributed with CalorieApp are included only for the documented application context. Third-party names, marks and assets remain subject to their respective rights and licences.

Before adding or replacing an asset, maintainers must privately verify its source, creator permission, licence, attribution requirements and compatibility with distribution. Repository possession or Git history alone does not prove ownership.

See `TRADEMARKS.md`, `NOTICE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
