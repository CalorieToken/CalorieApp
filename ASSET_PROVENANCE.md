Before adding or replacing an asset, maintainers must privately verify its source, creator permission, licence, attribution requirements and compatibility with distribution. Repository possession or Git history alone does not prove ownership.

## Recipe-family illustrations — 22 September 2026

The 18 WebP files in `frontend/public/images/recipes/` were generated with the
OpenAI image-generation tool for this CalorieApp update and resized to 384 px.
They cover broad dish families and can be shared by multiple recipe variants.
They are labelled illustrations/examples, not photos of a measured recipe,
merchant item or source provider's product. No OFF/USDA affiliation, certification
or exclusive third-party trademark right is claimed. Source-generation records
belong to the work session; the committed optimized WebPs are release artifacts.
