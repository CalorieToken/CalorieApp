# Contributions and intellectual-property terms

CalorieApp is publicly viewable but is not generally licensed as open source.
Do not submit code, documentation, designs, data, or other material unless you
have the right to do so and the project owner has agreed in writing to review
the contribution.

## No implied acceptance or transfer

- Opening an issue or pull request does not mean a contribution is accepted.
- A submission does not automatically transfer ownership to ICTHendrikse.
- No contribution will be merged when authorship, provenance, third-party
  licensing, employer rights, or assignment terms are unclear.
- Contributors must disclose AI-assisted or generated material and the human
  review performed.

## Required written terms

Before an external contribution is merged, the contributor and ICTHendrikse
must agree in writing whether the contribution is:

1. assigned to ICTHendrikse; or
2. licensed to ICTHendrikse under explicit project-compatible terms.

The agreement must also confirm that the contributor has authority to grant
those rights and that all third-party material is identified.

## Required submission information

- contributor's legal identity and capacity;
- authorship and source history;
- employer or contractor ownership implications;
- third-party files, code, data, fonts, or assets and their licences;
- AI tools materially used and the scope of human authorship/review; and
- any patents, trade marks, database rights, confidentiality duties, or other
  restrictions that may apply.

Security vulnerabilities must not be submitted through a public issue. Follow
`SECURITY.md` instead.

Use `docs/CONTRIBUTOR_RIGHTS_DECLARATION.md` to gather contribution provenance
and `docs/ASSET_CREATOR_DECLARATION.md` for visual or other creative assets.
These templates do not replace the separate written assignment or licence.
