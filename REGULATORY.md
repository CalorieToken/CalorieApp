# Regulatory boundary

The current CalorieApp V2 is presented as a non-financial, non-custodial food
and nutrition application. It does not
provide wallet custody, transaction signing, payments, token transfers, trading,
investment services or financial advice.

External identity may be used only for authentication context. Any future financial, token, reward, health-profiling or regulated feature requires separate legal, privacy, security and architecture review before public claims or implementation.

This summary is not legal advice and does not claim certification or regulatory approval.

## 22 September 2026 — voluntary pilot and pricing preparation

An isolated implementation candidate now prepares merchant stock/sales, consumer
purchase expenses and separately confirmed diary linking. It is not connected
to public routes, the live database or a signing provider. Mainnet and Testnet
pilot activation both remain false. The requested separate CalorieApp payment
rail has not been verified; website Xaman credentials are not a fallback.

Available CalorieApp features remain free of subscription and platform transaction
fees. Future premium options and fees are preparation only; no billing, automatic
renewal or retroactive debt is enabled. External network/provider charges remain
separate. Functional legal assessment, privacy, security and operator eligibility
are required for the actual payment service before public release; participation
and wallet signing do not themselves establish an exemption.
