# Regulatory boundary

Last updated: 25 August 2026

This notice describes the intended boundary of the software in this repository.
It is not a legal opinion, regulatory approval, or representation that any
separate CalorieToken activity complies with Regulation (EU) 2023/1114
(`MiCA`) or other financial-services law.

## Current CalorieApp V1

CalorieApp V1 is a non-financial, non-custodial food and nutrition
application. Its implemented scope is food search, nutrition display, and
authenticated user-scoped food logging and deletion.

Xaman/XUMM and XRP Ledger data are used only as external identity context in
the current V1. No wallet custody or financial transaction layer is claimed in
V1. The application does not, as part of its current implemented scope:

- safeguard or administer crypto-assets or private keys;
- exchange crypto-assets for funds or other crypto-assets;
- execute, receive, transmit, or place orders for crypto-assets;
- operate a crypto-asset trading platform;
- provide transfer services, portfolio management, or advice on crypto-assets;
- offer, sell, or solicit investment in CAL or another crypto-asset.

Adding any such functionality requires a separately approved architecture and
an appropriate legal, regulatory, privacy, and security assessment before
implementation or public release.
