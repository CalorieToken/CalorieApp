# Regulatory boundary

The current CalorieApp V2 is presented as a non-financial, non-custodial food
and nutrition application. It does not
provide wallet custody, transaction signing, payments, token transfers, trading,
investment services or financial advice.

External identity may be used only for authentication context. Any future financial, token, reward, health-profiling or regulated feature requires separate legal, privacy, security and architecture review before public claims or implementation.

This summary is not legal advice and does not claim certification or regulatory approval.
