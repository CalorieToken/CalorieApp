# Public roadmap

CalorieApp is being developed progressively, with public updates released at
reviewed milestones.

V2 starts from a proven functional baseline: the Render-hosted food-search,
nutrition, logging and sign-in experience was deployed and manually tested. Its
active completion order is baseline preservation; durable PostgreSQL and
recovery safety; multi-source food-data foundations; complete Identity Bridge
functionality, privacy, content and layout; eleven-language integration;
historically faithful website-wide polish; one integrated acceptance round; then
showcase preview, review and explicit scheduled publication approval.

V3 is reserved for a later complete Web3 application and ecosystem. No V3
architecture, database or launch promise is made during V2.

Optional XRPL provenance and ecosystem extensions remain disabled research
until their privacy, security and regulatory gates pass. Ideas that have not
been intentionally announced or released are not represented as implemented,
scheduled or guaranteed.
