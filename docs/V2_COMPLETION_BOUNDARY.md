# V2 completion boundary

Decision date: 2026-08-30.

## Version decision

The CalorieApp that ran on Render and was tested during development is the
proven V2 baseline. V2 remains the active product version until the complete
direction discussed for the integrated app, database, Identity Bridge,
localization and historically faithful website has been finished and reviewed.

Calling the baseline proven recognizes that the integrated application worked
at the tested point in time. It does not certify indefinite retention,
production recovery, privacy readiness or worldwide regulatory compliance.

`DS-3` means Data Safety step 3; it is not product version V3.

## V2 completion order

1. Preserve the exact working commit or immutable artifact, redacted deployment
   shape, dated smoke evidence and known URL status before changing integration.
2. Secure the WordPress route, embed and CalorieApp regression baseline.
3. Enforce abuse, capacity, retry and unwanted-mutation guardrails across the
   app, Identity Bridge, source adapters and future ecosystem clients.
4. Publish the free-evolution boundary: independent namespaced development is
   allowed, while official adoption and mutation stay versioned and reviewed.
5. Complete PostgreSQL migrations, persistence, backup/restore, portable export,
   erasure and retention gates before real public history depends on the service.
6. Add the source-independent catalog through a forward migration and put Open
   Food Facts behind its first versioned adapter. A second source need not be
   enabled yet, but adding one later must not require rebuilding the core model.
7. Finish Identity Bridge as one prepared whole before repeated manual testing:
   authentication and recovery, voluntary profile/name use, login widget,
   personal website areas, donation-purpose separation, consent, privacy,
   security, content and layout.
8. Apply the eleven-language contract to the app, bridge, website and external
   publishing platforms. Historical text-bearing images get translated variants
   while preserving their original composition, colors, fonts and visual identity.
9. Polish shared website behavior and layout while preserving historical header
   images, backgrounds, uploaded assets, typography, yellow/green/blue palette,
   near-square subtle rounding and historical button forms. Include cookie
   notice, menu, floating buttons, roadmap and deprecated exchange links.
10. Run automated regressions first, then one efficient integrated manual
   acceptance round instead of repeatedly testing incomplete pieces.
11. Finish with showcase preview, review, explicit approval and scheduled
   publication.

The source-neutral table portion of step 6 is implemented through migration
`20260901_0011`, including read-only provenance/licence evidence, bounded
internal ingest of new quarantined assertions with validated lineage, terminal
internal assertion moderation and retained internal correction. Open Food Facts
catalog persistence, authenticated public assertion mutation and any
second-source activation remain disabled pending their separate gates.

Market/How-to-Buy and optional XRPL provenance stay read-only or disabled unless
their separate privacy, MiCA and jurisdiction reviews permit the exact feature.

## Reserved V3

V3 is reserved for a genuinely new, complete Web3 application and ecosystem
generation after V2. No database technology is selected for it. BigchainDB may
be reconsidered later, but the current assessment does not select it because it
adds MongoDB, Tendermint and multi-node operations alongside XRPL and complicates
private-data erasure and permanently free operation.

The existing project DAO-votingtool may be inventoried, reviewed and shown in
V2 as an isolated non-binding preview using synthetic test data. This is an
optional enhancement and cannot delay the V2 release path. Real holder voting,
governance authority and a full DAO implementation are reserved for V3 and
still require a separate human-approved design, audit and activation decision.

The machine-readable boundary is
`contracts/release/v2/completion-boundary.json`.
