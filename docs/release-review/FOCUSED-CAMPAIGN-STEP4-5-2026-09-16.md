## Besluit en scope

De brede campagne is voor deze run vervangen door twee actieve lijnen:

1. `C2` — vijfjarig Jubileum op 16–17 september 2026;
2. `C1` — CalorieApp, het Calorie-ecosysteem en CalorieToken op WordPress.

`C3` Gebruik en doelgroepen, `C4` Geschiedenis en ontwikkeling en `C5`
Community Growth zijn ongewijzigd uitgesteld tot volgende week. Er is geen
inhaalpublicatie, geen tokenprijs-/beleggingsclaim en geen vermenging met
uitgestelde campagneonderdelen.

### B-P01 — Instagram / Facebook

The picture may look familiar, but the variant can still be wrong.

Before choosing a portion in CalorieApp, compare three things:

1. product name;
2. exact variant;
3. full barcode.

A successful scan confirms that a code was read. It does not by itself prove
that the returned product is the package in your hand.

Try one familiar product: https://calorietoken.net/calorieapp/

`#CalorieApp #FoodData #CalorieToken`

### C1-T02 — LinkedIn

One nutrition total does not mean every source supplied the same fields.

CalorieApp keeps Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central and other diary records
distinguishable. An A–E Nutri-Score letter is counted only when Open Food Facts
actually supplies it; CalorieApp does not invent a USDA score.

That source boundary is small on screen and important in meaning.

https://calorietoken.net/2026/09/21/how-calorieapp-keeps-food-sources-clear/

`#CalorieApp #OpenFoodFacts #FoodData`

### C1-T03 — Instagram / Facebook

A missing product photo should not become a misleading one.

When an Open Food Facts image is missing, broken or outside the allowed source,
CalorieApp uses a clearly recognisable local illustration. The image supports
identification; the product name, source and barcode remain the details to
verify.

See the food journey: https://calorietoken.net/showcases/

`#CalorieApp #FoodData #CalorieToken`

### C1-T04 — LinkedIn

One ecosystem, three clear roles:

- WordPress provides public context, guidance and the project archive.
- CalorieApp handles product search, portions, source-aware nutrition and the
  personal food log.
- CalorieHelp explains the step a visitor is viewing.

Current functions, historical experiments and future ideas stay distinct.

https://calorietoken.net/2026/09/23/one-ecosystem-calorieapp-wordpress-caloriehelp/
