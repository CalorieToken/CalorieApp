# CalEco revenue model — working proposal, 19 September 2026

Status: discussion, no prices, revenue splits, paid service or treasury activated.

The long-term purpose is to fund continuing CalorieEcosystem development and
eventually a defined ecosystem DAO through useful services. The user clarified
that this is specifically about the **business/revenue model**, not just how a
DAO votes. Participation remains voluntary.

## Proposed model: accessible core, optional paid services

| Candidate | Potential customer | Value they would pay for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Managed CalorieDB API and integrations | Food apps, retailers, restaurants, F&B developers | Reliable access, normalization, search, integrations, provenance and support |
| Data-quality and provenance services | Suppliers, catalog operators, F&B organizations | Validation, review workflows, traceable corrections and understandable reports |
| Managed public-data storage and deterministic compute | Developers or organizations choosing the managed service | Availability, verified task results, coordination and support |
| Optional platform marketplace service, later | Eligible adult creators and buyers who choose that service | Discovery, transaction handling interfaces and agreed platform service features |

These are candidate offers, not established demand or existing product claims.
Start validation with a small managed food-data/API or data-quality offer; build
on demonstrated willingness to pay before adding infrastructure. The simulator
does not yet support selling node reliability, retention or compute services.

The value proposition is reliable useful service. Publicly accessible source
data does not become exclusive CalEco property. Source licences, attribution and
the existing component rights still apply to any data/API offering. Private food
logs, identities and session data are not a revenue source.

Free ordinary app use, core game exploration and non-participation remain viable.
Storage, compute, reward claims and any NFT/crypto experience remain separate
opt-ins. A paid managed service does not require users of the core experience to
pay or run a node. Independent compatible applications remain subject to the
existing published contract/licence boundaries.

## How node participation fits

Real customers may eventually pay for a useful service that participating nodes
help deliver. The system must measure verification, coordination, replication
and operating costs; volunteer capacity is not automatically a cost saving or
reliable commercial service.

During testing, optional node/game rewards use CALT only. More verified useful
work can earn more bounded CALT, while ordinary use and the welcome allocation
remain independent of node contributions. CALT supply is a test-economy decision,
not business income. There is no current CAL/XRP reward payout.

## Revenue, operating costs and DAO funding

Keep separate records for customer revenue, delivery costs, support/maintenance,
development budget and any amount specifically allocated to a future ecosystem
treasury. Establish that allocation explicitly; no revenue percentages or asset
transfers are assumed here. The receiving operating entity and accounting model
remain to be chosen before any real offer is launched.

Voluntary donations, grants or disclosed sponsorship could supplement the model;
recurring customer value is the proposed foundation. Issuing test tokens, raising
their nominal reference valuation, or circulating rewards does not create revenue.

Any future platform fee applies to a chosen managed service. This proposal does
not add a compulsory fee to ordinary CAL transfers, modify the blackholed Mainnet
issuer, or require a crypto purchase to enter the ecosystem. Current real-value
adapters remain disabled.

## DAO direction and continuity

Future scoped DAO involvement may help decide ecosystem development priorities
and treasury budgets. Follow `contracts/ecosystem/v2/evolution-guardrails.json`:
the current slice is synthetic only; real governance remains a later, separately
designed stage with defined scope and accountable execution. CALT rewards do not
automatically grant binding governance power. No authority, official product
ownership, private data access or treasury ownership is transferred by this note.
