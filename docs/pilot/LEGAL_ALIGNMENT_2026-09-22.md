# Pilot, food features and future pricing — legal alignment draft

Prepared 22 September 2026. **Not legal approval and not a published replacement
of the live notices.** No real-money pilot or paid feature is activated by these
texts. The operator's identity, contact details and existing mandatory rights
must be retained when approved additions are applied to the current live pages.

## Scope and functional assessment

The present app provides food discovery and private logging. A proposed voluntary
merchant/consumer pilot would add purchasing records, stock administration and
payment verification, with signing through a separate app-owned rail. Those
are changes in function, data use and possibly regulatory scope, not merely a
new login option. Consent, a Testnet label or signing in Xaman does not determine
whether an activity is a regulated service. Mainnet eligibility must be assessed
for the actual operator, jurisdictions, service roles and payment flow before
activation. No MiCA, payment-services, sanctions or other exemption is asserted.
The [AFM CASP explanation](https://www.afm.nl/nl-nl/sector/cryptopartijen/vereisten-en-vergunningen/casp-vergunning)
lists the regulated crypto-asset service categories, including transfer services.

## Draft consumer and merchant terms

Participation would be optional for both the merchant and consumer, separately
for practice and real payments. Food search and diary access must not require a
CAL purchase. Before agreeing to one purchase, the parties need a clear price,
asset/network/issuer, recipient, applicable taxes, all platform and known external
fees, expiry and confirmation status. A displayed fiat estimate is informational;
it is not fiat settlement, a guaranteed conversion or a tax valuation.

The merchant is the seller of the food and must provide its own identity,
applicable transaction terms, receipts, product/allergen information and a
contact for complaints, returns or refunds. CalorieApp must not suggest that a
ledger payment certifies a seller, product, label, origin, sustainability, animal
welfare or food safety. Statutory consumer rights are not waived. A refund must
not be described as an on-chain reversal or triggered without a fresh approved
process. No global refund period or tax treatment has been invented.

Testnet XRP and any later verified CALT are practice units without monetary
value. Keep practice stock, revenue, purchases and diary entries distinct from
real records. A reset can remove external Testnet history; do not promise
convertibility, redemption or entitlement to Mainnet funds.

## Current free phase and possible future premium

Available CalorieApp functions are currently free of subscription and CalorieApp
transaction charges. This is not a statement that external wallets, issuers,
providers or blockchain networks charge nothing. No platform fee is accumulated
for later collection. There is no automatic paid renewal or hidden end date.

Potential future optional premium functions or platform transaction fees have
no announced price. Introduction requires a clear new offer, full applicable
price and tax/fee information, a separate explicit acceptance and the required
cancellation and refund arrangements. Merely continuing to use the current free
app or accepting pilot participation is not agreement to future charges. Keep
privacy requests and data export accessible without a paid plan. Applicable
pre-contract and additional-payment requirements must be assessed under the
[Consumer Rights Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj/eng)
and its current national implementation; this draft does not settle exceptions
or classify every future service.

## Privacy changes needed before collecting pilot records

| Proposed information | Purpose and access boundary | Release work still required |
|---|---|---|
| Verified wallet/network, participation and consent version | Network-specific account participation and one requested payment; no full-wallet scan | Identity proof, lawful basis, controller/processor roles and withdrawal process |
| Merchant products, stock movements, prices and optional cost price | Merchant inventory and sales administration; cost price private to merchant | Merchant access controls, staff roles, export and business retention schedule |
| Private invoice, public transaction reference and validation result | Reconcile a particular purchase, prevent replay; not a public account lookup | Retention by purpose, disputes/refunds, provider disclosure, account erasure behavior |
| Consumer purchases and reference-currency amounts | Private food/drink expense history | Consumer export/erasure and dated valuation/source disclosure |
| Explicitly selected consumed food/portion | Optional private diary entry; purchasing is not eating | Separate confirmation, no seller access to the diary, special-category assessment where applicable |

No food, receipt contents, profile, nickname or stable app account ID is included
in a payment memo. The candidate uses a random invoice reference. Public
addresses, amounts and transaction data remain on the ledger; removing a private
association cannot erase a blockchain transaction. Pseudonymous identifiers can
still be personal data when linkable. The proposed private link must not become
a public search interface.

Do not reuse transaction consent for marketing, food profiling, premium billing
or unrelated linking. Do not claim that agreeing to participate supplies every
required lawful basis. Purposes, minimization, basis, transparency, data-subject
rights, processor arrangements, security and any necessary DPIA must be assessed
against the actual flow under the [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng).
No new cookie, marketing tracker, contact-list import or food-data resale is
introduced by the pricing notice or isolated pilot candidate.

## Retention and deletion

The existing consumer-account policy cannot automatically be applied to shared
merchant invoices or business tax records. Specify controller responsibilities,
applicable statutory business retention and narrow legal-hold exceptions before
collecting real pilot data. Do not promise instant removal of another party's
lawfully retained invoice or of a ledger transaction. Conversely, do not retain
a consumer's diary under a merchant's accounting exception. Practice records
need their own documented period and reset/export behavior. No new production
retention schedule or erasure job is enabled here.

## Food/source and image wording

The released recipe catalogue offers 80 base titles with seven measured variants
each (640 named choices), not 640 independently verified traditional recipes.
Nutrition is an ingredient-based estimate with a disclosed portion/basis.
Illustrations are generated food-family examples, not documentary photos of a
specific recipe or packaged product. OFF, USDA and merchant-entered data retain
separate source attribution and quality limitations. A broader alternative is
a comparison idea, not medical advice, an allergy guarantee or a certified
healthier/environmental/animal-welfare claim. Labels must state their actual
source; absence of a label is not proof of its opposite.
